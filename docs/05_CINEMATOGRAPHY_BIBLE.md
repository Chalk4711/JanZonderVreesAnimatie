# CINEMATOGRAPHY BIBLE

## Philosophy
The camera is a storyteller.

## Core Influences
- Blue Eye Samurai
- Arcane
- The Last of Us

## Rules
- Prefer motivated camera movement.
- Use scale to communicate power.
- Use intimate framing for emotional scenes.
- Avoid random movement.

## Signature Shots
- Harbor reveal shots.
- Fish market life shots.
- Grandmother-and-Jan emotional closeups.
- Low-angle authority shots.
- Wide Flemish landscape shots.

## Camera Language
Safety = stable.
Danger = dynamic.
Wonder = slow reveal.
Belonging = warm close framing.

## Production Rule
Every scene should have a dominant emotional camera intention.
