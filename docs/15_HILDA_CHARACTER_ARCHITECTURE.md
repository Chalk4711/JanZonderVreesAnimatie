# HILDA CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

Status: Canon Draft

## Core Identity

Hilda is not a love interest.

Hilda is a co-protagonist with her own agency, intelligence, contradictions and emotional arc.

## Archetype

The Equal.

She challenges Jan not because she dislikes him, but because she sees through his performance of bravery.

## Core Desire

Self-determination.

Hilda wants to define herself beyond the expectations of family, farm, community and gendered assumptions.

## Core Fear

Dependence.

She fears needing others, being underestimated and losing control over her own life.

## Shadow

Control.

Hilda often uses sharpness, restraint and self-reliance to avoid vulnerability.

## Transformation

Season 1 moves Hilda from:

I can only trust myself.

toward:

Trust is not weakness when it is chosen consciously.

## Symbolic Meaning

Hilda represents reason, clarity and grounded judgment.

In relation to Jan, she is not the opposite of adventure. She is the force that asks whether adventure has meaning.

## Relationship to Jan

Jan + Hilda = possibility + reason.

Their relationship should be built through friction, respect and shared risk, not instant romance.

## Relationship to Stansen

Stansen represents responsibility and inherited expectations. Hilda respects him but resists being contained by his worldview.

## Visual Direction

- Practical Flemish farm clothing.
- Active posture.
- Observing eyes.
- Strong silhouette without fantasy warrior styling.
- No princess dress.
- No glamour corset design.

## Camera Language

The camera often observes Hilda before it follows her.

She reads the room. She assesses danger. She notices what Jan misses.

## Canon Rule

Hilda must always have a meaningful point of view in scenes where she appears. She must never exist only to validate Jan.
