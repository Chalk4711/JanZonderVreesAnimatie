# OPENING SEQUENCE PRODUCTION PACK

Status: Canon

## Purpose

Define the most important sequence in the project: the opening introduction to Jan's world.

## Narrative Goal

Introduce:

- the world;
- the community;
- the family;
- the protagonist.

In that order.

## Canon Route

Scheldt
→ Harbor
→ Fish Market
→ Krabbenstraatje
→ Neeltje's Fish Shop
→ Dried Plaice Sign
→ Attic Window
→ Jan

## Emotional Progression

Wonder
→ Curiosity
→ Familiarity
→ Belonging
→ Character Introduction

## Camera Strategy

Slow cinematic drift.

Audience discovers the world before meeting Jan.

## Sound Strategy

World first.

Water.
Gulls.
Ropes.
Market life.
Domestic sounds.
Only then music.

## Visual Strategy

Morning light.
Layered depth.
Living city.
Warm realism.

## Success Test

The viewer should already care about the world before Jan speaks a single line.
