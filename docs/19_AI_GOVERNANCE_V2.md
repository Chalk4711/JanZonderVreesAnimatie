# AI GOVERNANCE V2

Status: Canon

## Purpose

Ensure consistency across all AI-generated story, image, sound, animation and production assets.

## Core Rule

AI may create.
AI may expand.
AI may refine.

AI may not change canon.

## Canon Hierarchy

1. PROJECT_MASTER_CONTEXT
2. CANON_DECISION_LOG
3. CHARACTER_BIBLE and CHARACTER_ARCHITECTURES
4. VISUAL_STYLE_BIBLE
5. CINEMATOGRAPHY_BIBLE
6. SOUND_BIBLE
7. PROMPT_BIBLE
8. Episode-specific documents

Higher documents override lower documents.

## Consistency Validation

Before accepting an AI output, ask:

1. Does it fit the Project Master Context?
2. Does it fit the Character Bible?
3. Does it fit the Visual Style Bible?
4. Does it fit the Cinematography Bible?
5. Does it fit the Sound Bible?

If the answer is no to any major category, the asset is not canon.

## Character Lock Rule

Approved characters may be refined.
Approved characters may not be redesigned.

## Asset Rule

Whenever possible, approved canon assets should be used as reference inputs for future generations.

## Human Authority

The project lead remains the final authority for all canon decisions.

## Goal

Every generated asset should feel unmistakably like Jan Zonder Vrees.
