# JAN CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

Status: Canon Draft

## Core Identity

Jan is not a hero.

Jan is a boy who wants to become one.

## Archetype

Beginning: Reckless Youth

Destination: Responsible Protector

## Core Desire

Freedom.
Adventure.
A larger life.

## Core Fear

Being ordinary.
Being insignificant.

## Shadow

Jan mistakes recklessness for courage.

## Transformation

Season 1 teaches Jan that courage is responsible action despite fear.

## Symbolic Meaning

Jan represents possibility and the future.

## Relationships

Neeltje = Home

Hilda = Reason and challenge

Dokus = Loyalty and friendship

Stansen = Responsibility and adulthood

## Visual Direction

Early Jan:
- restless movement;
- impulsive body language;
- asymmetry.

Later Jan:
- greater stability;
- deliberate choices;
- stronger posture.

## Canon Rule

Jan's greatest early antagonist is not a villain.
It is his own immaturity.
