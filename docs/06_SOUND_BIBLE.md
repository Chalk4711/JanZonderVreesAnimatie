# SOUND BIBLE

Status: Canon v2

## Philosophy

Sound is an emotional storytelling layer, not decoration.

The rule is:

World first. Music second.

The audience should hear the world before they are told what to feel.

## Core Influences

- The Last of Us: restraint, intimacy, emotional economy.
- Blue Eye Samurai: tension, silence, controlled percussion and atmosphere.
- Klaus: warmth, memory, human softness.

## Music Direction

The score should feel organic, historical and emotionally direct without becoming museum music.

Preferred elements:

- strings;
- low drones;
- woodwinds;
- subtle choir;
- frame drum or restrained percussion;
- plucked strings;
- Flemish or Low Countries folk influence where appropriate.

Avoid:

- generic Hollywood fantasy bombast;
- modern pop language;
- overly clean trailer music;
- comic medieval pastiche.

## Sonic Identity of Antwerp

Antwerp should have a living acoustic identity:

- water against wood and stone;
- gulls;
- rigging and ropes;
- ship bells;
- market vendors;
- footsteps on wet stone;
- carts;
- distant church bells;
- wind through narrow streets.

## Location Sound Palettes

### Scheldt

Flow, depth, openness, destiny.

### Harbor

Ropes, masts, sails, gulls, bells, trade, foreign voices.

### Fish Market

Voices, knives, baskets, water, fish, barter, laughter, urgency.

### Krabbenstraatje

Narrow echoes, familiar voices, doors, footsteps, domestic life.

### Neeltje's Fish Shop

Interior warmth, wood, knife on board, cloth, quiet work, kettle, breath.

### Free Forest

Wind, leaves, distant birds, creaking wood, silence, unseen movement.

## Character Themes

### Jan

Energetic, forward-moving, unfinished.

His theme may begin with restless strings or folk rhythm and gradually gain emotional depth as he matures.

### Hilda

Clear, intelligent, observant.

Her sound world should be precise rather than sentimental: woodwind, light strings, restrained motifs.

### Neeltje

Warmth, memory and home.

Her theme should feel old, simple and emotionally honest. It may use plucked strings, low strings or soft human voice.

### Dokus

Loyal, gentle, open-hearted.

His theme should be modest and warm, never comic by default.

### Stansen

Earth, labor, responsibility.

Low strings, wood, weight and grounded rhythm.

## Silence Philosophy

Silence is not absence.

Silence is pressure, grief, attention or awe.

Use silence especially for:

- emotional realization;
- fear before reveal;
- Jan understanding consequences;
- Neeltje letting go;
- the moment before supernatural presence becomes visible.

## Folklore Sound Rule

The supernatural should usually be heard before it is seen.

Preferred order:

1. environmental disturbance;
2. character reaction;
3. unnatural silence;
4. motif or texture;
5. partial visual reveal;
6. full reveal only when earned.

## Final Sound Test

A sound cue belongs to Jan Zonder Vrees if it feels:

- organic;
- emotionally motivated;
- rooted in place;
- restrained when needed;
- warm when human;
- unsettling when folkloric;
- never generic fantasy.
