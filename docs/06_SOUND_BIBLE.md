# SOUND BIBLE

## Philosophy
Sound is an emotional storytelling layer, not decoration.

## Influences
- The Last of Us
- Blue Eye Samurai
- Klaus

## Music Direction
Organic instruments.
Strings.
Woodwinds.
Subtle choir.
Flemish historical influences where appropriate.

## Emotional Themes
Home.
Loss.
Belonging.
Courage.
Wonder.

## Sound Design
Naturalistic ambience.
Detailed market life.
Harbor textures.
Weather as storytelling.

## Rule
Silence is a tool and should be used intentionally.
