# PROJECT VISION

Status: Canon v1

## Core Statement

Jan Zonder Vrees is geen verhaal over een held die avonturen beleeft. Het is een verhaal over een jongen die leert wat moed, verantwoordelijkheid en thuishoren werkelijk betekenen.

## Why This Series Exists

The project exists because the underlying questions remain timeless:

- Who am I?
- Where do I belong?
- What does courage mean?
- When does a child become responsible?
- How do we let go of people we love?

The series uses medieval Flanders, Antwerp, folklore and adventure as the surface layer. The deeper layer is human growth.

## Genre

Coming-of-age historical adventure drama with warmth, folklore, humor, emotion and cinematic scope.

## Audience Direction

Accessible for older children and families, but written with adult emotional intelligence. The working target is: 12+ accessible, mature in craft.

## Emotional Promise

The audience should return for the world, but stay for the people.

## Creative Benchmark

- Blue Eye Samurai: cinematography, atmosphere, mature staging.
- Klaus: warmth, painterly light, emotional accessibility.
- Arcane: character depth and visual polish.
- Wolfwalkers: poetic contrast between city and wildness.

## Success Criteria

A scene is successful when it advances at least one of:

- character growth;
- emotional relationship;
- world understanding;
- theme;
- visual identity.

A beautiful scene that does not serve story or character is not enough.
