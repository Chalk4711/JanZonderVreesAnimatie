# SEASON 1 OVERVIEW

Status: Canon Draft

## Season Thesis

Season 1 is the story of a boy who mistakes fearlessness for courage and gradually learns responsibility.

## Character Arcs

### Jan
Freedom -> Responsibility

### Hilda
Self-reliance -> Trust

### Neeltje
Protection -> Letting Go

### Dokus
Following -> Choosing

## Themes

- Courage
- Belonging
- Family
- Identity
- Letting Go
- Responsibility

## Emotional Goal

The audience should feel connected to the people and world, not only impressed by the adventure.

## Season End State

Jan is no longer merely adventurous. He has begun to understand the cost of courage and the weight of responsibility.
