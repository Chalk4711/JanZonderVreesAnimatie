# VOICE AND DIALOGUE BIBLE

Status: Canon

## Philosophy

Dialogue reveals character before it explains plot.

The audience should learn who a character is from how they speak, what they avoid saying and when they remain silent.

## Jan

- Curious
- Direct
- Impulsive
- Occasionally overconfident

Jan often speaks before fully thinking.

## Hilda

- Observant
- Precise
- Practical
- Independent

Hilda notices details others miss.

## Neeltje

- Warm
- Grounded
- Wise
- Emotionally honest

Neeltje speaks from experience rather than authority.

## Dokus

- Loyal
- Open-hearted
- Honest

## Stansen

- Practical
- Responsibility-focused
- Economical with words

## Humor Rule

Humor emerges from personality and situation.

Avoid modern internet humor, meta jokes and contemporary slang.

## Emotional Rule

In important scenes, silence, expression and action should carry part of the meaning.

## Final Test

If character names are removed from a scene, readers should still be able to identify who is speaking.
