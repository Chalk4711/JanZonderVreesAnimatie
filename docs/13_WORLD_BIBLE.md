# WORLD BIBLE

Status: Canon Draft

## Philosophy

The world is not a backdrop.
The world is a character.

Every major location carries emotional meaning.

## Antwerp

Identity:

Warm.
Busy.
Human.
Commercial.
Alive.

Antwerp represents opportunity, community and the larger world calling to Jan.

## The Scheldt

Represents:

- change;
- destiny;
- movement;
- possibility.

The river is one of the most important recurring visual motifs.

## Harbor

Represents:

- freedom;
- trade;
- adventure;
- uncertainty.

Sound identity:
water, ropes, gulls, ships, bells.

## Fish Market

Represents:

- life;
- work;
- community;
- survival.

The market should always feel active and lived in.

## Krabbenstraatje

Represents:

- neighborhood;
- familiarity;
- belonging.

This is Jan's emotional home territory.

## Neeltje's Fish Shop

Represents:

- safety;
- roots;
- memory;
- family.

Color direction:
warm wood, ochre, amber light.

## Free Forest

Represents:

- mystery;
- danger;
- growth;
- transformation.

The forest should feel emotionally different from the city.

## Visual Rule

Locations should remain recognizable through silhouette, atmosphere, color and sound.

## World Rule

The audience should feel they could navigate the city after spending time with the series.
