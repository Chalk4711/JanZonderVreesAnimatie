# DOKUS CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

Status: Canon Draft

## Core Identity

Dokus is Jan's most loyal friend.

## Archetype

The Faithful Friend.

## Core Desire

To belong.

## Core Fear

Being left behind.

## Shadow

Following others instead of choosing his own path.

## Transformation

Following -> Choosing.

## Symbolic Meaning

Dokus represents loyalty.

## Relationship to Jan

Dokus admires Jan but must gradually develop his own courage and judgment.

## Emotional Function

Dokus often reflects how ordinary people experience events around Jan.

## Canon Rule

Dokus should never exist purely as comic sidekick. His loyalty must have emotional weight.
