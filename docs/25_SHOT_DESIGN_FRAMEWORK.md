# SHOT DESIGN FRAMEWORK

Status: Canon

## Purpose

Define how shots are designed across the series.

## Core Rule

Every shot must answer:

- What is the audience feeling?
- What is the character feeling?
- What changed before and after this shot?

## Shot Hierarchy

1. Emotion
2. Character
3. Story
4. Composition
5. Historical Detail

## Signature Shot Families

### Home Shots
Warm framing, practical light, shared space.

### Harbor Shots
Scale, movement, possibility, layered depth.

### Market Shots
Energy, density, community.

### Forest Shots
Mystery, obstruction, atmosphere.

### Authority Shots
Symmetry, distance, low-angle power.

## Composition Principles

- Foreground layers
- Windows and doorways
- Ropes and beams
- Atmospheric depth
- Readable silhouettes

## Canon Rule

Beautiful composition must never override emotional clarity.
