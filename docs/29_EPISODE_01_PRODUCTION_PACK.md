# EPISODE 01 PRODUCTION PACK

Status: Canon Draft

## Purpose

Provide the foundational production package for the pilot episode.

## Episode Function

Introduce:

- Jan
- Neeltje
- Antwerp
- Harbor Life
- Core Themes

## Emotional Objective

The audience should feel connected to Jan's world before the first major adventure begins.

## Core Themes

- Belonging
- Curiosity
- Courage
- Family
- Possibility

## Required Locations

- Scheldt
- Harbor
- Fish Market
- Krabbenstraatje
- Neeltje's Fish Shop

## Required Character Moments

### Jan
Demonstrates curiosity and impulsiveness.

### Neeltje
Demonstrates warmth and protective instinct.

### Dokus
Demonstrates loyalty.

### Hilda
Demonstrates intelligence and independence.

## Visual Goals

Audience immediately understands visual identity of the series.

## Sound Goals

Audience immediately understands acoustic identity of Antwerp.

## Success Criteria

Audience wants to spend more time in the world after the episode ends.
