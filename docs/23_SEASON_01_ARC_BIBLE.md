# SEASON 01 ARC BIBLE

Status: Canon

## Season Thesis

Season 1 is the transformation of Jan from a boy seeking freedom into a young person beginning to understand responsibility.

## Surface Story

Adventure, folklore, harbor life, mystery and discovery.

## Deeper Story

Identity.
Belonging.
Family.
Growing up.
Letting go.

## Jan Arc

Beginning:
Freedom.

Middle:
Consequences.

End:
Responsibility.

## Hilda Arc

Beginning:
Self-reliance.

Middle:
Shared risk.

End:
Trust.

## Neeltje Arc

Beginning:
Protection.

Middle:
Fear of loss.

End:
Acceptance and letting go.

## Dokus Arc

Beginning:
Following.

Middle:
Doubt.

End:
Choice.

## Emotional Destination

The audience should finish the season feeling attached to the people and the world, not merely entertained by events.
