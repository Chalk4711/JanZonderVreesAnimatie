# BOER STANSEN CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

Status: Canon Draft

## Core Identity

Boer Stansen is the responsible adult world that Jan must learn to understand.

He is not a villain and not merely an obstacle. He represents duty, labor, land, order and the weight of practical life.

## Archetype

The Responsible Adult.

## Story Function

Stansen shows Jan what adulthood looks like when it is grounded in responsibility rather than romantic adventure.

## Symbolic Meaning

Stansen represents responsibility.

In the character network:

Jan = possibility
Hilda = reason
Neeltje = home
Dokus = loyalty
Stansen = responsibility

## Relationship to Hilda

Stansen is Hilda's father and represents inherited expectations. He loves her, but he may underestimate the scale of her inner life and ambition.

## Relationship to Jan

Jan + Stansen = freedom + responsibility.

Stansen should challenge Jan not because he hates him, but because he distrusts unfocused impulsiveness.

## Visual Direction

- Around 50 years old.
- Strong Flemish farmer.
- Broad shoulders.
- Heavy work hands.
- Practical work clothing.
- No fantasy barbarian styling.
- No nobleman styling.
- No 19th-century farmer look.

## Emotional Function

Stansen grounds the story. When he appears, questions of consequence, labor, family and responsibility enter the scene.

## Canon Rule

Stansen must never be reduced to a simple authoritarian father. He represents a real worldview with dignity, limitations and emotional weight.
