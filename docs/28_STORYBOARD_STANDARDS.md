# STORYBOARD STANDARDS

Status: Canon

## Purpose

Ensure storyboards communicate emotion, story and cinematography consistently.

## Required Information Per Panel

- Shot type
- Camera intention
- Emotional intention
- Character focus
- Dialogue (if applicable)
- Sound note (if relevant)

## Emotional Priority

A technically correct storyboard that lacks emotion is incomplete.

## Camera Priority

Every panel must support the Cinematography Bible.

## Character Priority

Every panel must support the Character Architecture documents.

## Visual Rule

Storyboard clarity is more important than artistic detail.

## Canon Test

Can someone unfamiliar with the scene understand:

- who matters;
- what they feel;
- what changed?

If not, revise the panel.
