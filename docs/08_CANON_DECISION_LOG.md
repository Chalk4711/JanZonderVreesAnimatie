# CANON DECISION LOG

## Purpose
Single source of truth for major creative decisions.

## CD-001
Target quality level is Premium Streaming Animation.
Status: Approved.

## CD-002
Historical authenticity is the foundation, not the prison.
Status: Approved.

## CD-003
Visual balance follows the spirit of Blue Eye Samurai, Arcane and Klaus.
Status: Approved.

## CD-004
Project uses an AI-first production pipeline.
Status: Approved.

## CD-005
Character Lock is mandatory before large-scale asset generation.
Status: Approved.

## CD-006
GitHub is the canonical AI knowledge base. Notion remains the human-facing workspace.
Status: Approved.

## CD-007
Approved canon characters may be refined but not redesigned.
Status: Approved.

## CD-008
The series is not primarily about heroism.
The series is about growing up.
Heroism emerges as a consequence of growth.
Status: Approved.

## CD-009
Hilda is not a supporting love-interest character.
She is a co-protagonist with independent goals, agency and emotional development.
Status: Approved.

## CD-010
Neeltje is the emotional heart of Season 1.
Status: Approved.

## CD-011
Jan's first major antagonist is his own immaturity.
Status: Approved.

## CD-012
Emotional truth takes priority over historical perfection, while maintaining historical plausibility.
Status: Approved.

## CD-013
Jan Zonder Vrees is a character-driven, historically inspired, cinematic animation series.
Status: Approved.

## CD-014
The project's core visual style is Poetic Realism.
Status: Approved.

## CD-015
The official visual formula is:
40% historical reality
40% cinematic readability
20% emotional enhancement.
Status: Approved.

## CD-016
The opening sequence follows the canonical route:
Scheldt -> Harbor -> Fish Market -> Krabbenstraatje -> Fish Shop -> Plaice Sign -> Attic Window -> Jan.
Status: Approved.

## CD-017
World first. Music second.
Sound design leads emotional immersion before score.
Status: Approved.

## CD-018
The supernatural should be heard before it is seen whenever possible.
Status: Approved.

## CD-019
Older characters must visibly remain their age. Neeltje must always read as 70+.
Status: Approved.

## CD-020
Every major location must possess a distinct emotional, visual and sonic identity.
Status: Approved.
