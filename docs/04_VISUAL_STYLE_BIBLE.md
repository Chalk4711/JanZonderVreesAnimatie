# VISUAL STYLE BIBLE

Status: Canon v2

## Visual North Star

A living Flemish painting filmed with the cinematic discipline of Blue Eye Samurai and Arcane, warmed by the emotional humanity of Klaus.

The series should feel historically grounded, emotionally legible and visually desirable.

## Core Style Term

Poetic Realism.

Definition:

The world feels real, but it is filmed as memory, not as documentation.

## What This Is Not

- Not museum reconstruction.
- Not generic fantasy.
- Not Disney simplification.
- Not grimdark medieval realism.
- Not modern cosplay in medieval clothes.

## Visual Formula

40% historical reality
40% cinematic readability
20% emotional enhancement

## Reference Balance

### Blue Eye Samurai
Use for:
- cinematic framing;
- restraint;
- atmosphere;
- mature staging;
- graphic composition.

Do not copy:
- Japanese visual identity;
- revenge-thriller tone;
- extreme adult violence.

### Klaus
Use for:
- warmth;
- painterly light;
- human softness;
- accessible emotional appeal.

Do not copy:
- overtly Christmas-card stylization;
- overly rounded caricature proportions.

### Arcane
Use for:
- production polish;
- character texture;
- expressive faces;
- painterly surfaces.

Do not copy:
- steampunk technology;
- neon urban palette;
- overly modern aggression.

### Wolfwalkers
Use for:
- contrast between city and wildness;
- emotional use of shape language;
- poetic relationship to nature.

Do not copy:
- flat Celtic graphic style as primary look.

## Color Philosophy

### Antwerp / Community
Warm earth, ochre, muted brick, aged wood, linen, smoke, market color.

### Harbor / Scheldt
Blue-grey water, pale sky, rope brown, sail cream, wet stone.

### Neeltje's Shop
Amber, candlelight, old wood, fish-silver, hearth warmth.

### Hilda / Farm World
Moss green, soil brown, linen cream, practical natural dyes.

### Authority / Civic Power
Cold stone, desaturated red, metal grey, controlled gold.

### Forest / Mystery
Deep green, mist blue, shadow brown, moonlit silver.

## Light Philosophy

Warm light = belonging.

Cold light = danger, distance or authority.

Mist = uncertainty.

Backlight = transformation.

Candlelight = intimacy and memory.

## Material Philosophy

Materials must feel touchable:

- wool;
- linen;
- leather;
- wood;
- rope;
- wet stone;
- fish scales;
- iron;
- smoke;
- mud.

The viewer should sense weight, temperature and texture.

## Character Design Rules

- Strong silhouettes.
- Readable at thumbnail size.
- Distinct age groups.
- Clothing communicates work, class and region.
- Faces must carry lived experience.
- Beauty comes from specificity, not polish alone.

## Age Rule

Older characters must remain visibly old.

Neeltje must read as 70+ in every approved design. She may be warm and strong, but never glamourized into a 50- or 60-year-old.

## Costume Rule

Costumes are historically inspired and emotionally functional.

They must avoid:

- fantasy armor unless narratively justified;
- modern tailoring;
- decorative excess without story purpose;
- generic medieval RPG design.

## Environment Rule

Every environment must communicate:

1. where we are;
2. who lives there;
3. what emotional state the story is in.

## Final Visual Test

An image belongs to Jan Zonder Vrees if it feels:

- Flemish;
- lived-in;
- cinematic;
- emotionally warm;
- historically plausible;
- visually memorable.
