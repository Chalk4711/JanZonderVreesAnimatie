# VISUAL STYLE BIBLE

## Visual North Star
A premium animated historical drama.

Primary inspirations:
- Blue Eye Samurai (cinematic staging)
- Arcane (character depth)
- Klaus (warmth and painterly light)

## Style Principles
- Painterly realism.
- Historically inspired, not museum reconstruction.
- Emotional readability before historical perfection.
- Rich silhouettes.
- Natural materials.

## Character Design Rules
- Strong silhouettes.
- Readable at thumbnail size.
- Distinct age groups.
- Clothing communicates social position.

## Color Philosophy
Warm human interiors.
Cool danger and authority.
Golden memory and belonging.

## Historical Authenticity Rule
Authenticity is the foundation.
Stylization is permitted when it improves storytelling and emotion.
