# NEELTJE CHARACTER ARCHITECTURE

Status: Canon Draft

## Core Identity

Neeltje is the emotional heart of Season 1.

She is grandmother, caregiver, protector and keeper of memory.

## Archetype

The Hearth.

## Core Desire

To protect Jan.

## Core Fear

To lose Jan.

## Shadow

Overprotection.

Her love sometimes becomes a desire to hold on too tightly.

## Transformation

Season 1 moves Neeltje from:

Holding on.

toward:

Letting go.

## Symbolic Meaning

Neeltje represents:

- home;
- roots;
- memory;
- continuity.

## Relationship to Jan

Jan is her future.

The emotional tension is not conflict but transition. She knows he must grow. She fears what growth will cost.

## Visual Direction

- 70+ years old.
- Strongly readable age.
- Warm expression.
- Weathered working hands.
- Practical fish seller clothing.
- Historically inspired Flemish dress.

## Emotional Rule

The audience should immediately feel safe when entering Neeltje's space.

## Location Connection

Neeltje's fish shop functions as an extension of her character.

## Canon Rule

Neeltje is not comic relief. She is one of the primary emotional anchors of the series.
